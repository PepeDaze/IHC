## Brand & Style

This design system serves urban transit concessionaires and fleet operators navigating route management, subscription dues, and municipal concessions. The visual identity merges systematic utility with high-legibility operations design: an aesthetic rooted in functional modernism and clarity under intense operational tempo.

The emotional signature is dependable, authoritative, and direct. Concessionaires manage cash flow, driver assignments, and vehicle compliance under strict deadlines; the interface eliminates ambient noise, frivolous gradients, and ambiguous interactions in favor of crisp administrative density and uncompromising accessibility. High-contrast typography, structural panels, and unmistakable operational states provide certainty across sunlight-drenched dispatch terminals and mobile inspections.

## Colors

The palette leverages a high-contrast chromatic structure tuned for enterprise administrative workflows:

- **Primary Blue (`#1A5BCE`)**: Primary actions, interactive indicators, active navigation nodes, and active subscription status markers.
- **Deep Slate Canvas / Typography (`#0A111A`)**: High-contrast body typography, desktop navigation rails, and data table headers.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary foreground surfaces, card containers, contextual sheets, and data entry matrices.
- **Neutral Steps**:
  - `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50): Global application backdrop canvas.
  - `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100): Subtle structural dividers, alternating table row shading, and component borders.
  - `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200): Inactive field boundaries and card perimeters.
  - `#334155` (Slate 700): Meta labels, secondary descriptors, and auxiliary table column values.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - **Success Emerald (`#047857`)**: Validated concessions, on-time fee collections, and active routes. Exceeds WCAG AAA contrast requirements against light backgrounds.
  - **Warning Amber (`#B45309`)**: Expiring concessions, routine inspections due, and pending balance reconciliations.
  - **Error Crimson (`#B91C1C`)**: Disciplinary suspensions, delinquent route dues, and mechanical out-of-service alerts.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes legibility and swift scanning over decorative flourishes.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Handled via `Plus Jakarta Sans`, utilizing structured weights (600/700) for metric displays, module heads, and dialog banners.
- **Body & Data Grid Cells**: Utilizes `Inter` for regular text hierarchies, ensuring distinct glyph identification and neutral legibility across multi-column data views.
- **Labels, Metrics, & Badges**: Set in `Inter` with medium-to-bold weights (600/700) and optical tracking adjustments (+0.02em to +0.04em) to preserve readability on status pills and metadata rows.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture relies on a fluid 12-column grid on desktop screens, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet devices and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 240px fixed side-rail for primary navigation; persistent content area spanning a 12-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Side-rail collapses to an icon drawer; 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and gutters. Desktop tabular grids transform into stacked operational cards.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Layout flows use consistent multiples of `0.25rem` (4px), reserving `space-md` (`1rem`) for standard component interiors and `space-xl` (`2rem`) for major functional section separation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy shadows and skeuomorphic blurs, utilizing tonal separation and structural low-contrast borders for elevation:

- **Level 0 (App Canvas)**: Surface background tinted with Neutral Slate 50 (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Sidebars)**: Elevated onto pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` or `#F1F5F9`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Filter Menus, Dropdowns)**: White surfaces bounded by `#E2E8F0` paired with an ambient, low-contrast shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 17, 26, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(10, 17, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays, Critical Dialogs)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` floating over a backdrop wash of `#0A111A` at 48% opacity, styled with a defined border (`#E2E8F0`) and an elevated structural drop: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 17, 26, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 17, 26, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

Shapes feature a restrained, soft profile (`roundedness: 1`), reinforcing precision and maximum spatial efficiency:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cells)**: `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius for sharp geometric alignment.
- **Containers (Cards, Modal Sheets)**: `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- **Pill Elements (Status Badges, Filter Chips)**: Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`) exclusively used for status indicators, micro-metrics, and categorical filters to distinguish dynamic states from clickable square action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#1A5BCE` fill, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), height 40px, horizontal padding `1rem`, `rounded` (4px). Focus ring: `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[#1A5BCE] focus-visible:ring-offset-2`.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, `#0A111A` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive**: Solid `#B91C1C` fill or outlined 1px `#B91C1C` border with `#B91C1C` text for irreversible route/driver terminations. Focus ring: `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[#B91C1C] focus-visible:ring-offset-2`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height 40px, pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, internal text `#0A111A`, placeholder `#334155`. Labels must always render outside the input field in `#334155` (`label-md`).
- **Focus State**: `border-[#1A5BCE]` with `ring-2 ring-[#1A5BCE] ring-offset-2`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px x 18px dimensions, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, checked fill `#1A5BCE`, displaying an explicit SVG check/dot with standard high-visibility focus offset ring.

### Data Tables
- **Header**: `#0A111A` background with `#FFFFFF` text (`label-md`), uppercase tracking, height 44px, horizontal cell padding `1rem`.
- **Rows**: `#FFFFFF` alternating with `#F8FAFC`, delimited by 1px `#F1F5F9` bottom border. Cell vertical padding minimum `12px` (generous row spacing for rapid scanning).

### Badges & Status Chips
Always combine a clear SVG icon with explicit text; color alone cannot convey state:
- **Paid / Active**: `#047857` icon and text on a 10% `#047857` tint background with full pill radius.
- **Pending / Expiring**: `#B45309` icon and text on a 10% `#B45309` tint background.
- **Delinquent / Inactive**: `#B91C1C` icon and text on a 10% `#B91C1C` tint background.

### Cards & Panels
- White `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` radius, internal padding `1.5rem`.
- Header section partitioned with a 1px bottom divider `#F1F5F9` separating operational actions from detail contents.
